# The names

The code is free to use, change and fork under its MIT license. The names **VIABLE** and **VETTING**
identify this project and the work behind it, so people can tell what they are getting.

You may, without asking:

- say your product "runs on the VIABLE harness" or "uses VETTING from the VIABLE harness" when it
  does;
- say your implementation "reads VIABLE's formats" or "implements VETTING" when it passes the
  matching suites in `conformance/`;
- use the names to refer to this project in articles, talks and teaching.

Please don't:

- name a fork, product or service "VIABLE …" or "VETTING …", or use the names in a way that suggests
  the VIABLE Lab made, reviewed or endorses it;
- say a deployment is "checked by VETTING" when replies can reach learners without passing it.

Questions: open a discussion or write to a maintainer (MAINTAINERS.md). This is a policy, not a
registration.

## Evidence

**What its authors showed.** Standards and their learning components as their publishers wrote them; see Learning Commons’ documentation.

**What comparisons in the harness showed.** Choosing a standard needs Learning Commons’ API (VIABLE’s hosted platform has it); the harness carries the standard as chosen. Not yet compared with and without in this harness.

**What nobody has shown yet.** Whether the standard in the AI’s instructions changes its replies or what learners learn.

## Evidence

**What its authors showed.** A framework of skills and levels from the Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching; see its documentation for how it was developed.

**What comparisons in the harness showed.** Carried as chosen in an activity’s teaching settings; not yet compared with and without in this harness.

**What nobody has shown yet.** Whether naming a skill and level in the AI’s instructions changes its replies or learners’ growth in the skill.

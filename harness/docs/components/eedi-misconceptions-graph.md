## Evidence

**What its authors showed.** Eedi’s misconceptions are drawn from its diagnostic questions in mathematics; see Eedi’s and Learning Commons’ documentation for how they were made and checked.

**What comparisons in the harness showed.** Matched to a teacher’s words by shared terms, because the open release doesn’t link constructs to standards; not yet compared with and without in this harness.

**What nobody has shown yet.** Whether listening for chosen misconceptions changes the AI’s replies or what learners learn.
